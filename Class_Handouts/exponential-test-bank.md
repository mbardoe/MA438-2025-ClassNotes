# Exponential Functions – Test & Review Question Bank

## Section 1: Simplifying Expressions with Powers

1. Simplify:  
   $\left(\dfrac{x^3 + x^2}{x}\right)^2$

2. Simplify:  
   $\dfrac{4a^{-2} - 2a^{-1}}{2a^{-3}}$

3. Simplify:  
   $(3a^2b^3)^2$

4. Simplify:  
   $\left(\dfrac{x^2 - x^{-1}}{x^3}\right)^{-2}$

5. Simplify:  
   $\dfrac{9^{-2} + 27^{-1}}{3^{-3}}$

## Section 2: Growth Rate Word Problems

6. A car is worth $22,000 and depreciates by 12% per year.  
   - Write a function for the value after $t$ years.  
   - How much is the car worth after 5 years?

7. A population of 1,500 bacteria grows by 8% each hour.  
   - How many bacteria will there be in 4 hours?  
   - When will the population reach 3,000?

8. A stock is growing at 6.5% per year. You invest $5,000.  
   - How much will your investment be worth in 10 years?  
   - What is the equivalent growth factor?

9. A rare book increases in value by 4.2% annually.  
   - How much will it be worth in 15 years if it is worth $3,200 now?  
   - What percent increase is this overall?

## Section 3: Doubling Time and Half-Life

10. A virus doubles every 3 days. 20 people are infected now.  
    - How many will be infected in 7 days?  
    - Write a function for this situation.

11. A radioactive substance has a half-life of 12 hours.  
    - How much remains after 27 hours from an initial 10g?  
    - When will only 1.25g remain?

12. A chemical doubles every 2.5 minutes, starting at 0.8g.  
    - Write the equation.  
    - How much is present after 6 minutes?

13. A town grows exponentially with a doubling time of 18 years, starting at 25,000 people.  
    - When will the population reach 60,000?  
    - Estimate the population after 25 years.

## Section 4: Graph Interpretation

14. Rank by growth rate:  
    $f(x) = 2^x$, $g(x) = 1.5^x$, $h(x) = 3^x$

15. Which functions show decay?  
    $f(x) = 0.9^x$, $g(x) = 1.05^x$, $h(x) = 2^{-x}$, $k(x) = 5^{x-2}$

16. Which function has the smallest base?  
    $f(x) = 4^{x/3}$, $g(x) = 2^x$, $h(x) = (1.2)^{2x}$

## Section 5: Functions Through Two Points

17. $A(0) = 50$, $A(4) = 80$  
    - Find $A(t)$ in the form $A_0(1 + r)^t$

18. A sample is 200g at $t = 0$ and 100g at $t = 3$  
    - Write the decay function and determine the half-life

19. Population is 150,000 in 2000 and 195,000 in 2010  
    - Write an exponential model  
    - Predict population in 2025

20. Population is 180 in year 5, 360 in year 9  
    - Write the function  
    - Estimate year 12 population

## Section 6: Compounding and Continuous Growth

21. $1,000 at 6% annual rate. How much after 10 years:  
    - Compounded quarterly  
    - Compounded monthly  
    - Compounded daily  
    - Compounded continuously

22. Compare:  
    - Plan A: 5.5% monthly  
    - Plan B: 5.25% continuous  
    - Which gives more in 20 years on $10,000?

23. 4.8% rate:  
    - How much more earned from continuous compounding vs. annual over 30 years?
