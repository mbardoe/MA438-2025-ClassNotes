# Lesson: Exponents and Logarithms – Mixed Review Worksheet

## Topics Covered

### Review Topics: Chapter 5 – Exponents and Logarithms

#### 5.1 – Exponential Functions

- Understand the concept of growth as a percent (the $r$ value)
- Laws of Exponents
- Simplify exponential expressions using laws of exponents

---

#### 5.2 – Exponential Functions and Rational Exponents

- Work with rational exponents
- $1/n$ as the $n^\text{th}$ root
- Know that $b^0 = 1$ and $b^1 = b$
- Solve exponential equations by rewriting with the same base
- Simplify exponential expressions

---

#### 5.3 – Modeling with Exponential Functions

- Write exponential functions to model real-world scenarios (population, half-life, bank loans, etc.)
- Convert between $(1 + r)^t$ and $b^t$ form
- Understand how $b^{t/d}$ form relates to half-life and doubling scenarios
- Find an exponential function $f(x) = ab^x$ given two points on the curve
- Understand the graph of $ab^x$ for growth and decay, including:
  - Effect of the $b$ value on shape (steepness, growth vs. decay)
  - Equation of any asymptotes (given as a line)
  - Y-intercept
  - Significance of $f(0) = a$
  - Transformations
  - Domain and Range

---

#### 5.4 – The Number $e$

- Know the decimal approximation of $e$ ($e \approx 2.718$)
- Know the limit definition of $e$
- Understand the effect of compounding interest more frequently
- Understand the concept of effective annual interest rate
- Know the compound interest formula: $A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}$
- Know the continuous compounding formula: $A = Pe^{rt}$

---

#### 5.5 – Logarithms

- Understand logarithms as the inverse of exponentials
- Know the relationship: $b^x = y \iff \log_b(y) = x$
- Understand the graph of $\log_b(x)$ and its relationship to $b^x$
- Be able to evaluate logarithmic expressions
- Know the natural logarithm ($\ln$) and common logarithm ($\log$)

---

#### 5.6 – Laws of Logarithms

- Know the Laws of Logarithms:
  - $\log_b(xy) = \log_b(x) + \log_b(y)$
  - $\log_b\left(\frac{x}{y}\right) = \log_b(x) - \log_b(y)$
  - $\log_b(x^p) = p \cdot \log_b(x)$
- Use log rules to simplify expressions and solve equations

---

#### 5.7 – Solving Equations, Change of Base

- Know the Change of Base Formula:  
  $\log_b(x) = \frac{\log(x)}{\log(b)}$ or $\frac{\ln(x)}{\ln(b)}$
- Solve exponential and logarithmic equations using log rules
- Use the calculator’s log function appropriately
